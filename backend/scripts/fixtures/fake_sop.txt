# 1
Purpose and scope. This procedure covers the operator response to Motor_1 trip
alarms on the primary conveyor drive. It applies to all shifts.

# 2
Initial operator response. Upon receiving any Motor_1 alarm, acknowledge the
alarm on the HMI and open the Motor_1 trend screen before taking any action.

# 3
Overload response. If the Motor_1 trip alarm is accompanied by an overload
condition, first check the VFD (Variable Frequency Drive) load percentage on
the HMI trend screen. If VFD load exceeded 100% for more than 30 seconds prior
to the trip, this confirms an overload condition. Do not attempt to reset the
motor immediately. Instead, inspect the conveyor for mechanical jam or
excessive material buildup, clear any obstruction, and allow the VFD load to
return to a normal baseline before resetting Motor_1 from the HMI.

# 4
Escalation. If the overload recurs within 15 minutes of reset, or if no
mechanical obstruction is found, escalate to the shift maintenance lead and
do not attempt a second reset without their approval. Log the event in the
shift handover notes.
